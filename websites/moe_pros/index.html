About moe_pros

Our Story & Commitment

moe_pros is a proud, locally run business established right here in Gillette, Wyoming. We are passionate about creating beautiful, well-maintained lawns for our neighbors. Our focus is exclusively on residential properties within the Gillette area, ensuring we provide dedicated and personalized service to each suburban home we care for. We believe in strengthening our community, one perfectly manicured lawn at a time.

What Makes Us Different?

Local Expertise

Being based in Gillette, we understand the local climate, soil, and specific needs of yards in our community. We're not just a service; we're your neighbors.

Personalized Care

We treat every yard as if it were our own. Our team pays attention to detail, ensuring a consistent, high-quality finish every time. We don't do commercial properties, so our focus is entirely on your home.

Reliability You Can Trust

When you schedule with moe_pros, you can count on us to be there, on time, and ready to deliver exceptional service. Your satisfaction is our top priority.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and reliable lawn care provider for residential homes in Gillette, WY, known for our meticulous work, friendly service, and commitment to making every yard a source of pride for its owner. We aim to give you back your valuable time, allowing you to enjoy your beautiful outdoor space without the chore of maintenance.
