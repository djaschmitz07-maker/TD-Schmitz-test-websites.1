About GL Mowing

Welcome to GL Mowing, where green dreams come true! Founded by Gary L. Peterson (that's the 'GL'!), we've been serving the community for over 15 years with a commitment to excellence and customer satisfaction. Gary started this business with a single mower and a vision: to provide reliable, high-quality lawn care that makes every yard a masterpiece.

Our team of skilled professionals is passionate about transforming and maintaining beautiful outdoor spaces. We believe in sustainable practices, attention to detail, and building lasting relationships with our clients. From the smallest residential lawn to large commercial properties, GL Mowing treats every project with the care and precision it deserves. We're not just mowing lawns; we're cultivating beauty and enhancing curb appeal, one blade of grass at a time.

Our Expert Services

Discover the wide range of services GL Mowing offers to keep your property looking its best.

Mowing Services

Regular lawn mowing, edging, and trimming to keep your grass perfectly manicured.

Mowing Service Options:

  • Weekly Mowing: Our most popular plan! Enjoy a perfectly manicured lawn every week. Includes mowing, precise edging along driveways and walkways, and trimming around obstacles. Ideal for maintaining peak aesthetic and grass health during growing seasons.
  • Bi-Weekly Mowing: A great option for slower-growing lawns or those who prefer a slightly less frequent schedule. We'll ensure your lawn stays tidy and healthy every other week, with the same attention to detail as our weekly service.
  • Monthly Mowing: Perfect for properties with minimal growth or during off-peak seasons. This service provides essential maintenance to keep your lawn presentable and prevent overgrowth, ensuring it's ready for more frequent care when needed.
  • One-Time Mowing: Need a quick tidy-up for a special event or just to get things back in order? Our one-time service is flexible and efficient.

Landscaping Rocks

Enhance your garden beds and pathways with beautiful, durable rock installations.

Landscaping Rock Services:

  • Decorative Rock Installation: Choose from a wide variety of river rocks, lava rocks, pea gravel, and more to create stunning, low-maintenance landscape features.
  • Rock Pathway Creation: Design and install elegant and functional rock pathways that complement your garden's aesthetic and improve accessibility.
  • Erosion Control Solutions: Utilize strategically placed rocks and boulders to manage water flow and prevent soil erosion on sloped areas.
  • Rock Garden Design: Create unique rock gardens that add texture and visual interest, often incorporating drought-tolerant plants.
  • Rock Bed Maintenance: Keep your existing rock beds clean and weed-free with our professional maintenance services.

Yard Clean-Up

Seasonal clean-ups, leaf removal, and debris hauling to keep your yard pristine.

Yard Clean-Up Services:

  • Spring Clean-Up: Prepare your yard for the growing season by removing winter debris, dead leaves, and pruning overgrown shrubs.
  • Fall Leaf Removal: Comprehensive leaf collection and removal services to prevent damage to your lawn and maintain curb appeal.
  • Storm Debris Removal: Quick and efficient removal of branches, leaves, and other debris after severe weather.
  • General Debris Hauling: We'll remove unwanted yard waste, brush, and other organic materials, leaving your property spotless.
  • Gutter Cleaning: Ensure proper drainage and prevent water damage with our thorough gutter cleaning service.

Hedge & Shrub Trimming

Expert shaping and pruning for healthy, well-maintained hedges and shrubs.

Hedge & Shrub Trimming Services:

  • Precision Shaping: Our experts meticulously trim and shape hedges and shrubs to enhance their natural form and promote dense growth.
  • Seasonal Pruning: Timely pruning to remove dead or diseased branches, encourage flowering, and maintain plant health.
  • Overgrowth Management: Taming unruly hedges and shrubs to restore their aesthetic appeal and prevent obstruction.
  • Small Tree Pruning: Light pruning for smaller ornamental trees to improve structure and appearance.
  • Hedge Removal: If you're looking to clear space, we can safely remove unwanted hedges and shrubs.
